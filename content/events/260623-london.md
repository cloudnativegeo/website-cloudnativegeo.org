## Agenda

The Beacon at The Jellicoe · 5 Beaconsfield St, London N1C 4EW, UK

**08:00 · Breakfast, meet & greet**

### Opening

**09:00 · Welcome & introduction**<br>
Jed Sundwall

### Morning session

*15-minute talks with 5 minutes for Q&A.*

**09:15 · Featured talk: A World Without Public Data**<br>
Craig Mills · WRI<br>
*An overview of innovations and progress made possible by public data, and what we need to do to sustain them.*

**The GeoAI UK Outlook: So What?**<br>
Luca Budello · Innovate UK<br>
*Why technical readiness isn't enough — and what it takes to move GeoAI from prototypes to adoption.*

**Turning Earth Observation AI into Action for Environmental Problematics**<br>
Noelia Jiménez Martínez & Glen Low · Earth Genome<br>
*Earth Index — foundation-model embeddings making EO actionable for non-experts; emerging applications in seagrass / blue carbon.*

**Fields of the World: Mapping Every Agricultural Field on Earth**<br>
Isaac Corley · Taylor Geospatial<br>
*A planetary-scale geospatial AI pipeline from research breakthrough to deployable infrastructure.*

**10:30 · Coffee break**

**11:00 · TESSERA: Pixelwise Embeddings of Earth Observations**<br>
Anil Madhavapeddy · University of Cambridge<br>
*Global 10 m pixel-wise embeddings, open weights, Zarr v3 + STAC adoption — and new revised models.*

**Large Multidimensional Datasets Challenge for Cloud-Native Workflows**<br>
Sol Cotton · Open Climate Fix<br>
*A walk through how our Zarr chunking strategies have transformed access efficiency across multiple dimensions.*

**Elevating the Field — Getting Ground-Level Nature Data into the Cloud**<br>
Molly Bank · Echo Labs<br>
*Why it's hard, where we are now, and what we prioritize from here.*

**Barrios Visibles: Mapping Argentina's Informal Settlements with CNG**<br>
Nissim Lebovits · Radiant Earth
